01 — Reading the Site

Problems, potentials & behaviour

Dehiwala Junction sits at the convergence of the Galle Road corridor and the rail line — a dense, mixed-use node under continuous pedestrian and vehicular pressure.

P1

Congestion at the junction core. High betweenness values concentrate along Galle Road and the station approach, meaning through-traffic and local access compete for the same narrow carriageway.

P2

Fragmented open space. The Open Space Ratio (OSR) map shows very low ground-level open space in the commercial core, clustering what little exists near the periphery.

P3

Thermally stressed core. UTCI and surface heat readings peak over the paved commercial strip, where FSI is highest and tree canopy is thinnest.

P4

Uneven urban maturity. The Urban Maturation Index highlights a mature, high-intensity core against a rapidly transitioning residential edge to the east.

K1

Potential — strong local closeness at the 500m scale suggests the junction already works well as a walkable neighbourhood centre, if reclaimed from vehicle dominance.

K2

Potential — mixed land-use entropy is comparatively high near the station, a foundation for transit-oriented intensification.

02 — Space Syntax / sDNA
